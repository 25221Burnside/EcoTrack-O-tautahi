Which Bin Does It Belong In?

Search over 60 common household items below or review Christchurch City Council's 4-bin rules.

Quick Filters:

Yellow Bin

Clean Recycling

  • Rigid plastics labeled 1, 2, & 5
  • Clean paper, cardboard, & cartons
  • Aluminum & tin cans (rinsed)
  • Glass bottles & jars
Rinse all items thoroughly!

Green Bin

Organic Composting

  • Food scraps & leftovers
  • Lawn clippings & garden prunings
  • Meat, bones, & dairy products
  • Soiled paper towels & napkins
No plastic bags or treated wood.

Red Bin

Landfill Waste

  • Non-recyclable plastics (3, 4, 6, 7)
  • Disposable coffee cups & lids
  • Nappies & sanitary items
  • Broken ceramics & pyrex
Last resort for unrecyclable waste.

Special Drop-off

EcoDrop / Retail Hubs

  • Soft plastic packaging
  • Batteries & household electronics
  • Paint, chemicals, & oils
  • Lightbulbs & fluorescent tubes
Take to nearest EcoDrop center.

Project Justification

Kaitiakitanga & Technical Alignment

How this digital outcome aligns with assessment criteria, usability standards, and cultural guardianship.

1. Functionality

Features interactive location search, Leaflet.js mapping, an expanded 60+ waste item lookup database, Christchurch suburb bin schedules, footprint savings tracking, and RSVP event registration.

2. Aesthetics

Designed with an earthy NZ-inspired palette (#1b4332, #52b788), high-contrast Plus Jakarta Sans typography, rounded card layouts, and responsive spacing across all mobile and desktop viewports.

3. IP & Data Credits

Data structured according to Christchurch City Council (CCC) waste guidelines, OpenStreetMap base tiles, registered non-profit organization links, and FontAwesome icons.
